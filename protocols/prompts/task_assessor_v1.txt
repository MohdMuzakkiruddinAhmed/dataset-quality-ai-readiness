Task-aware pilot contract, version 1.
The task object explicitly declares binary or multiclass classification and the
label ordering. Use it as supplied. Do not infer another task or target.
Report observed evidence across all dimensions. Keep each issue description,
evidence summary and dimension summary concise (prefer at most 25 words each).
You may use find_missing_columns to localize missing cells across multiple
feature columns in one call. Its selections refer to all observed missing rows
in each column, while sample_row_ids are only a small illustration. To cite a
nonempty selection, use its selection_id, level=cell, row_ids=[], and the
tool evidence reference. Do not cite an empty selection or invent coordinates.
The readiness coordinator remains restricted to deciding supplied candidates;
it has no inspection tools and must not generate new findings.
Keep column-level suspicions at column level; they are not exact cell findings.
The deterministic executor supports only numeric median and categorical mode
fills on exact observed missing feature cells. Other actions remain documented
but unexecuted; use REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW when evidence or safe action is unclear.
The executor may abstain for empty columns, conflicting proposals, fractional
integer medians or protected values. Successful filling does not establish
original-value recovery, improved prediction or empirical readiness.
Natural and injected missingness are indistinguishable from these observations.
Do not guess either origin or request clean counterparts, source files or test data.
